We are now officially changing our name from Serverless Stack to NEO. We are also moving our domain from [serverless-stack.com](https://serverless-stack.com) to [neo.khulnasoft.com](https://neo.khulnasoft.com).

We were previously called Serverless Stack and we were hosted at serverless-stack.com. Aside from being really long, it was also easy to confuse us with the Serverless Framework folks. We've been informally using NEO but now with the new domain, we can make the change official!

You can call us **NEO** and visit us at [**neo.khulnasoft.com**](https://neo.khulnasoft.com)!
